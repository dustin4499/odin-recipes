Let's fry us a hamburger!

Ingredients

  • Hamburger Meat (Your choice of ratio)
  • Frying Pan
  • American Cheese Singles
  • Potato Buns
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Your choice of condiments and spices

Steps

  1. Ball out 6 oz of your hamburger meat and mix with seasonings of your choice
  2. Pre heat frying pan on medium high
  3. Put patty in frying pan and smash
  4. Wait 3 to 4 minutes then flip
  5. when patty is done (around 160 degrees F) put american single on and turn off heat
  6. Put patty on bun and add lettuce tomato onion and whatever condiments you prefer
