Let's make some spaghetti!

Ingredients

  • Frying pan
  • Pot
  • Hamburger Meat (Your choice of ratio)
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Pasta Noodles

Steps

  1. Fill pot full of water and turn on high heat
  2. Once water is boiling place frying pan on stove eye put on medium high and fry hamburger meat
  3. After hamburger meat is finished drain pasta noodles from water and strain noodles
  4. put noodles back in pot add hamburger meat and tomato sauce
