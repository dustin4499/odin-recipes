Let's get into some tatertot casserole!

Ingredients

  • Hamburger Meat
  • Cream of Mushroom
  • Shredded Cheese (your preference)
  • Tater Tots
  • Casserole Dish

Steps

  1. Brown your hamburger meat on skillet
  2. Mix in your cream of mushroom and shredded cheese
  3. Bake your tater tots at 425 for 20 to 25 minutes
  4. Put your hamburger meat mixed with your cream of mushroom and shredded cheese in your casserole dish
  5. Top with tater tots and shredded cheese
  6. Bake at 350 for 15 minutes!
